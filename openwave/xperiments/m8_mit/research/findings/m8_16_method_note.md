## 1. Equations first

`R = c = g = 1`. Coefficients `c ∈ ℂᵐ` are stored in real form `x = (Re c, Im c) ∈ ℝ²ᵐ`, with the inner product `Re(cᴴd)`, and `J₀` is multiplication by `i`. `u = 2.22×10⁻¹⁶`, double precision's machine epsilon, as the code reads it.

### 1.1 The continuum problem

```text
ψ_TT − Δψ + |ψ|²ψ = 0,          ψ : S³ → W_ρ,   ψ(γx) = ρ(γ)ψ(x)  for γ ∈ 2I acting on the left
ψ = e^{iωT}φ,  λ = ω²:          F′(φ) = 0,   F(φ) = ½⟨(−Δ − λ)φ, φ⟩ + ¼∫|φ|⁴,   |·| the fibre norm at each point
ε = λ − λ₀                       λ₀ = 48 in R4 and R5, 63 in R2, 8 in R3
rotating frame, ψ = e^{iωT}(φ + ζ):   ζ'' + 2iω ζ' + L ζ = 0,   L ζ = (−Δ − λ)ζ + |φ|²ζ + 2 Re(φ†ζ) φ
x = (ζ, ζ'):     x' = A x,   A = [0, I; −L, −2ω J₀],   B = diag(L, I),   W = [2ω J₀, I; −I, 0]
                 Aᵀ W = B,   Ω(x, y) = xᵀ W y,   Ω(A x, y) = B(x, y)
Theorem K:       k_r + 2 k_c + 2 k_i⁻ = K = n(L) − n(D)
Theorem R(a):    the slow eigenvalues are ε(κ s τ + o(1)),   κ = 1/(8 √λ₀ Q(u)),   s = 9/16 in R5 and 1 otherwise
```

- **`L` is `F″(φ)`,** real-linear and symmetric for `Re⟨·,·⟩`.
- **The leading order** is in the terms' notation. `τ` is the frozen small-amplitude spectrum for unit `u` in `R4`'s normalization (the terms, § FROZEN VALUES). An imaginary pair's leading order is `i εκsτ`, a quartet's is `εκsτ` with `τ` its first-quadrant member, and a real pair's is `εκs√ν`.
- **Theorem R** holds for `0 < ε < ε₁`, with `ε₁` not stated, and it gives no size for the `o(1)` term at any finite `ε`. The terms list this among their stated limits.

### 1.2 The discretization

```text
basis, level n:   f_{n,i,k}(x) = √(n+1) A_i π_n(x) e_k,   n ≤ N
                  π_n the spin-n/2 representation, e_k its weight basis, A_i an orthonormal (Frobenius) basis of
                  Hom_2I(V_n, W_ρ): the group average of the elementary matrices in a fixed order, then Gram–Schmidt,
                  both applied twice
                  orthonormal in L² with the normalized measure;  −Δ = n(n+2) on level n
ψ = Σ_j c_j f_j:  F(c; λ) = ½ Σ_j (n_j(n_j+2) − λ)|c_j|² + ¼ Σ_q w_q |ψ(x_q)|⁴
the rule:         a = √t e^{iα},  b = √(1−t) e^{iβ};  4N+1 uniform nodes in α and in β, N+1 Gauss–Legendre nodes in t
                  exact for every monomial in (a, b, ā, b̄) of degree ≤ 4N, so F, F′ and F″ are exact to round-off
```

- **The cutoffs** are `N = 16, 24` and 32, and 40 for C1. In `R4` the basis has 50, 138 and 316 complex coefficients.
- **The symmetry (DERIVED, by Peter–Weyl).** The truncated space is invariant under right `SU(2)` and the fibre group: the phase, with charge conjugation, in `R3`, `R4` and `R5`, and `Sp(1)`, generated by `i` and the quaternionic structure `J`, in `R2`.
- **Not collocation.** The scheme is variational Galerkin: nothing is evaluated at nodes and interpolated.

### 1.3 The exact kernel

`F` is invariant to round-off on the truncated space. Differentiating that invariance twice at a critical point puts every orbit tangent in `F″`'s kernel to round-off (DERIVED): `T_aφ` for `a = 1, 2, 3`, `iφ`, and in `R2` also `Jφ` and `iJφ`. No zero is inserted, and no mode is projected away. A tangent leaves the kernel only through the residual, `‖L Tφ‖ = ‖T F′(φ)‖ ≤ ‖T‖ ‖F′(φ)‖`.

### 1.4 Standing waves: the bordered Newton

```text
unknowns (c, λ) at fixed m = ‖c‖²:   F′_λ(c) + Σ_j μ_j t_j = 0,   ‖c‖² = m,   ⟨t_j, c − c_slice⟩ = 0
t_j:        an orthonormal basis of the orbit tangents' span at the previous accepted point, its rank by pivoted QR
the stop:   once the residual is below 10⁻⁸ S, with S = ‖(−Δ)c‖ + λ‖c‖ + ‖P(|ψ|²ψ)‖, Newton steps while the
            residual halves, and keeps the smallest
accept:     the floor, the unbordered residual and the multipliers, each below 10⁻¹⁵ S
```

The borders act only in the solve. The spectrum is read on the unbordered `L`.

### 1.5 Seeds and continuation

```text
seed, ε = 0.25:   the case's lowest-level representative u, unit, scaled to m = ε/Q(u)
seed check:       Q(u) within 10⁻¹² of its exact value, relative;  criticality on the lowest level at most 10⁻¹²;
                  (k, r_u), from the orbit tangents and σ(x, y) = Re⟨x, iy⟩, the Result's
continuation:     in m, with ε an output: from the accepted point at ε_{k−1}, a secant in m, iterated until
                  |ε − ε_k| ≤ 10⁻⁶, at most 8 times
the end:          a slope that is not positive (a fold), a Newton failure, or a secant short of ε_k
the points:       ε ∈ {0.25, 0.5, 1, 2} at N = 16, 24 and 32, each cutoff on its own path from its own seed
C1:               Theorem D's exact branch, c = √ε v, λ = λ₀ + ‖c‖², with no solve, at ε ∈ {0.5, 1, 2} and N to 40
```

A failed seed ends its case before any solve, and the later cutoffs are recorded as not checked.

### 1.6 The linear record

```text
spectrum:   the dense eigendecomposition of A, of real size 4m
margin:     κ √(u‖A‖ max(m, m₀)), κ = 2, m₀ = 0.2;  the zero cluster |λ| < margin;  its gray zone (margin/G, G margin), G = 3
V₀:         the cluster's invariant subspace, from an ordered real Schur form; its dimension and the ranks of A and A²
            on it equal the case's expected V₀; it holds the exact symmetry directions, with their leak at most
            κ_L u‖A‖/gap, κ_L = 200, capped at 10⁻⁴
groups:     outside the cluster, eigenvalues linked within τ_g(λ) = max(margin, κ_d √(u‖A‖ |λ|)), κ_d = 10, each typed
            real, imaginary or complex by its centre; an imaginary group's Krein sign from B on its eigenvectors, or
            from its real Schur subspace at a defective group; a group carrying both signs is unresolved
n(L):       the inertia of L at its cut, 1000 u‖L‖, with its gray zone to 10 times it; its zero count equals V₀'s chains
n(D):       the inertia of B on V₀ at 10⁻⁸ ‖B‖;   K = n(L) − n(D) = k_r + 2k_c + 2k_i⁻, checked at every point
type:       Hyperbolic when a group off the axis by more than τ_g lies outside the gray zone and V₀ is as expected;
            Elliptic only when nothing is unresolved;  Unresolved otherwise
```

- **`τ_g`** is the terms' `τ(λ)`, renamed here so that it is not read as the frozen spectrum `τ`.
- **The calibration** was fitted on the controls. Outside their range the margin is an extrapolation, and `R5` lies outside the slots it was fitted on (the terms, § STATED LIMITS).

### 1.7 The convergence rule

```text
converged:  the type, n(L), K, V₀ and each imaginary group's negative Krein count are equal at every cutoff, and
            U ≤ d/10 for every tested group or set, d its distance to the nearest event
tested:     every typed group below half the band edge √(n₁(n₁+2)) − √λ, n₁ = 18 in R4 and R5, 17 in R2; every
            Krein-negative or off-axis group; and each tested imaginary group's nearest group of opposite Krein sign
matched:    one to one and both ways across the cutoffs, unique if the next candidate is at least twice as far;
            where that fails, the set rule: the group and its competitors of the same class and sign, as one set
U:          the largest of d₂²/(d₁ − d₂), the larger margin of the two highest cutoffs, and ‖P_g‖ max(‖R_g‖, u‖A‖)
events:     the cluster's gray zone, at |λ| − G margin;  the imaginary axis, at |Re λ|;  the nearest group of
            opposite Krein sign
```

Beside the rule, unscored, the relative change between the two highest cutoffs is reported against `10⁻⁶`, the fraction the rule retired as a gate.

### 1.8 The checks and the verdict

```text
slow band:  every group with |λ| ≤ 2 max |λ_pred(ε)|
leading:    each predicted mode matched to a computed group of its class and Krein sign, one to one, by the least
            total relative distance;  its deviation |λ − λ_pred| / |λ_pred|;  the bar 0.05 at ε = 0.25
K1:         every point accepted, V₀ as expected, the zero cluster within the margin and separated
K2:         the slow band holds exactly the predicted modes, and nothing outside the cluster lies in its gray zone
            (the pyramid, C2a and C2b)
K3(a):      at ε = 0.25, the point accepted and its seed checked; ‖L t‖ ≤ 1000 u‖L‖ ‖t‖ for every exact symmetry
            direction t, L's zero count equal to the chains and its gray zone empty; and, outside the in-span class
            (imaginary, Krein-negative), the slow band Theorem R's: the quartet and the Krein-positive pair within
            the bar, and no other group
K3(b):      K1 and K2 there; exactly one definite Krein-negative imaginary group in the slow band, outside the gray
            zone, within the bar of 0.25 κ τ_in
K3(c):      its static overlap at least 0.9 with span{T_xφ, T_yφ} and at most 0.1 with T_zφ
K3(d):      the group (b) finds at the highest cutoff passes the convergence rule
K4:         C1, Theorem D's answers; C2a, C2b, C3 and C2a/R5, converged with the expected type and indices, within
            the bar at ε = 0.25, and the deviation at 0.25 below that at 2; the pyramid at ε = 0.25, converged,
            Hyperbolic, (5, 1, 4), with its quartet and its Krein-positive pair within the bar
```

- **K3's outcome** is one of three. Resolved: (a) to (d) pass. Not resolved, with the kernel exact: (a) passes and (b), (c) or (d) fails, and the diagnosis is wrong. Instrument failure: (a) fails, and the diagnosis is untested.
- **The verdict.** The instrument qualifies if K1 to K4 pass on C1 to C3 and the pyramid. A failed check of C1 to C3 or the pyramid stops the run, the driver writes the gate record, and the verdict is NOT QUALIFIED, naming the failing check. The rows, scored by K5 and K6, run only on a gate record that shows K1 to K4 passed.
- **The bar's premise.** The terms read a disagreement with Theorems B, D and R at the smallest amplitudes as indicting the instrument, and they state that Theorem R does not give the range of `ε` in which its leading order holds.

### 5.1 The quartet's departure reads as a next-order term in `ε`
